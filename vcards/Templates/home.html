FAQ

Answers before you tap.

What is an NFC card?

An NFC card is a smart card that opens your digital profile when tapped on a supported phone.

Does it work on all phones?

It works on modern NFC-enabled phones, and every card can also include a QR option for wider compatibility.

What if the phone does not support NFC?

The person can scan your QR code or open your shareable profile link.

Do users need an app?

No. Tap2Connect profiles open in a normal browser.

Can I update my profile later?

Yes. You can update your links, contact details, CV, portfolio, or business content later.

Can I add my CV or portfolio?

Yes. Career and professional profiles can include CVs, projects, certificates, documents, and portfolio links.

Can I add products to my business page?

Yes. Business mini websites can include products, prices, photos, maps, hours, and WhatsApp ordering.

Do you support bulk school cards?

Yes. Tap2Connect supports school and bulk digital ID card workflows for institutions.
